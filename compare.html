IF YOU HAVE PETS

Brush design matters more than a big suction number.

Expect to clear hair from brushes over time. Anti-tangle designs can reduce that chore, but fur length, shedding, and floor type still matter.

IF YOU HAVE RUGS

Check how mops get out of the way.

A robot that lifts mop pads may still leave them close to a high-pile rug. Check pad lift and carpet behavior against your actual rugs.

IF YOU HAVE CORDS

Obstacle avoidance lowers risk; it doesn’t remove it.

Even advanced sensors can miss thin cables, flat objects, dark surfaces, and pet accidents. A little floor pickup still helps.

IF YOU WANT LESS WORK

Every “hands-free” dock still has a routine.

Dust bags, dirty-water tanks, mop trays, and filters need attention. Choose which chores you want the dock to take over.
