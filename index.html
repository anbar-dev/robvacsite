A QUICK REALITY CHECK

Three things worth checking before checkout.

A spec sheet can’t measure whether a robot will clear your sofa, fit beside a cabinet, or match your cleanup habits.

01

Measure the tightest gap

Check sofa and bed clearance against the robot’s height. Include the dock footprint and room to open its cover.

Think about your rugs
