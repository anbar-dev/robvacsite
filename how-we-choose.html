Who publishes DustMigo?

DustMigo is a small robot-vacuum buying guide maintained by its creator and published under the DustMigo name. Recommendations draw on U.S. manufacturer information and the independent product tests cited below. DustMigo has not personally tested these shortlisted models at home or in a lab; the site does not claim a named reviewer or testing team.

How we choose

We keep the list short and choose models that represent different household needs: a simpler auto-empty vacuum, an everyday vacuum-and-mop dock, a pet-and-carpet focused option, and a premium navigation-focused option.

We compare practical details that change ownership: brush maintenance, carpet behavior, mop design, object sensing, dock chores, footprint, and how much setup a feature adds. We look for an honest downside for every recommendation.

The finder filters out models above your budget reference tier. Asking for dock mop washing removes the Q10 S5+, which does not wash its mop. If you enter a measured furniture gap, the finder keeps only robots whose published body height leaves a 0.2-inch buffer; its listed heights are 3.90 in (Q10 S5+), 3.80 in (Qrevo S5V), 3.82 in (L40 Ultra Gen 2), and 3.14 in (Saros 10R). That buffer is a conservative DustMigo screening rule, not a fit guarantee. Uneven floors, thresholds, and furniture shape matter, so check the actual path. The remaining models are ordered by the priorities you selected for floors, mess, and upkeep. A close second is shown when it offers a useful alternative; tied choices are called out. We do not show compatibility percentages.

Your answers stay in the browser; the finder does not send or store them.

What this is (and isn’t)

We have not personally tested these models in a DustMigo lab or in our own homes. Our current recommendations are based on manufacturer specifications and product information, plus the practical limitations those features suggest. We do not describe them as hands-on reviews or claim test results.

The homepage hero is an AI-generated editorial illustration, not a photo of any shortlisted robot or a record of a DustMigo test. The product-card visuals are original footprint schematics scaled from published dimensions; they are not exact product renderings.

Manufacturer performance numbers are labeled as claims when used. A high suction figure cannot promise a specific result on your carpet, and object avoidance can fail on thin cables, small objects, or unusual conditions. We avoid live prices because retailer pricing and bundles can change.

The comparison page cites tests by outside reviewers for the exact model where we could verify them. Their methods and conditions differ, so results such as pickup percentages should only be read within that publisher’s test; they are not a shared DustMigo score or a direct cross-brand ranking. We also note when a test sample was supplied by a manufacturer or when detailed results are not public.

We do not publish customer reviews, ratings, or star averages. If we add original testing later, we’ll explain the setup and method on the relevant page.
